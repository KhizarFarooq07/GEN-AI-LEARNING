French Cuisine and Paris Dining

French cuisine is renowned worldwide for its sophistication, technique, and flavor. Paris, as the culinary capital of France, offers everything from street food to Michelin-starred fine dining.

Classic French Dishes:
- Coq au Vin: Chicken braised in red wine with mushrooms and pearl onions.
- Beef Bourguignon: A hearty beef stew cooked in Burgundy wine.
- Ratatouille: A vegetable stew from southern France.
- Cassoulet: A rich bean and meat casserole.
- French Onion Soup: A traditional soup topped with cheese and bread.
- Crêpes: Thin pancakes filled with sweet or savory fillings.
- Croissants and Pastries: Famous French baked goods available at every corner.

Food Prices in Paris:
Budget options: Street food and casual bistros offer meals for €8-15. Markets sell fresh produce and cheese. Boulangeries (bakeries) offer affordable pastries and sandwiches.
Mid-range: Traditional bistros and restaurants serve meals for €15-30 per person.
Upscale: Fine dining and Michelin-starred restaurants charge €60-200+ per person.

Wine Culture: Paris has a sophisticated wine culture with numerous wine bars (wine shops and tasting venues). French wines are an integral part of Parisian dining.

Café Culture: Sitting at a Parisian café with coffee and croissants is an essential experience. Cafés are social gathering places where locals and tourists spend hours relaxing.

Markets: Paris has beautiful food markets like Marché Bastille and Marché Aligre offering fresh produce, cheese, bread, and prepared foods.

Modern Dining: Paris also features modern restaurants with contemporary cuisine, fusion options, and increasingly diverse international food options.

Tokyo Sightseeing and Attractions

Senso-ji Temple:
One of Tokyo's most famous temples, founded in 645 AD. Located in Asakusa, it attracts millions of visitors annually. Features a 56-meter-tall red lantern as its iconic symbol. Entry free. Asakusa district preserves traditional Tokyo atmosphere with narrow streets and shops.

Tokyo Skytree:
A modern broadcasting tower standing 634 meters tall, second-tallest structure in the world. Observation decks on two levels offer 360-degree views. Admission: ¥2100-3100 ($14-21).

Tokyo Disneyland and DisneySea:
Theme parks located in Chiba prefecture (adjacent to Tokyo). DisneySea is unique to Tokyo and features a nautical theme. Daily admission: ¥8900-10900 ($60-73).

Meiji Shrine:
One of Japan's most important Shinto shrines, surrounded by a forested area. Located near Harajuku station. Entry free. A peaceful retreat from urban bustle.

Shibuya Crossing:
Famous intersection known as the world's busiest pedestrian crossing. No formal admission; best viewed from Starbucks overlooking the crossing or at pedestrian level. Free.

Tsukiji Outer Market and Toyosu Market:
Major wholesale markets offering fresh seafood, produce, and street food. Some sections are tourist-friendly. Entry typically free or minimal cost.

teamLab Borderless:
Digital art museum featuring immersive installations and interactive exhibits. Located in Odaiba. Admission: ¥3200 ($21).

Imperial Palace:
Former residence of Japanese emperors. East gardens open to the public. Entry free for gardens. Exterior viewing is free and impressive.

Shinjuku:
Major entertainment and business district with towering skyscrapers, shops, restaurants, and nightlife. Window shopping and exploring is free; specific venues have their own admission fees.

Tokyo National Museum:
Japan's oldest and largest museum with extensive collections of Japanese art and artifacts. Admission: ¥1000 ($7).

Roppongi Hills:
Modern commercial complex with shops, restaurants, art galleries, and observation decks. Entry free; individual venues have separate admission.

Transportation: Tokyo has one of the world's most efficient metro and train systems. A rechargeable IC card (Suica/Pasmo) costs ¥2000-4000 initial load. Ten-ride tickets or day passes are available at reasonable rates.

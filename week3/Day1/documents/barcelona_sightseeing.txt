Barcelona Landmarks and Sightseeing

Sagrada Familia:
A breathtaking basilica designed by Antoni Gaudí, started in 1883 and still under construction. UNESCO World Heritage site. Stunning architecture with intricate stone carvings and stained glass. Admission: €26-33. Interior ascent costs extra. Approximately 3-4 million visitors annually.

Park Güell:
Another Gaudí masterpiece, a large public park with colorful mosaics, curved balconies, and panoramic city views. UNESCO World Heritage site. Admission: €10 for the main section, free for the park periphery. Beautiful terraces and landscape design showcase modernism.

Casa Batlló:
An apartment building designed by Gaudí featuring wavey stone façade and colorful mosaic decorations. UNESCO World Heritage site. Admission: €27. Interior features surreal design elements.

Gothic Quarter (Barri Gòtic):
Medieval neighborhood with narrow streets, historic buildings, and Barcelona Cathedral. Entry to streets free; cathedral admission €7. Atmospheric and historic area perfect for exploration.

Barcelona Cathedral:
A stunning Gothic cathedral in the heart of the Gothic Quarter, begun in the 13th century. Admission: €7. Features a beautiful interior courtyard and roof with city views.

La Rambla:
A famous tree-lined boulevard running from Plaça Reial to the waterfront. Free to walk. Crowded with street performers, shops, and restaurants. Gateway to the Mediterranean.

Montjuïc:
A hill with museums, gardens, and cultural institutions. Many attractions have separate admission fees (€8-15 each). The Magic Fountain offers free evening light shows. Accessible by metro, funicular, or cable car.

National Art Museum of Catalonia (MNAC):
Situated on Montjuïc, displaying Catalan art from Romanesque to contemporary periods. Admission: €12.

Park of Spain (Parc d'Espanya):
A recreational park with attractions and viewing areas on Montjuïc. Entry free.

Montserrat:
A mountain sanctuary about 50km northwest of Barcelona featuring a Benedictine monastery and stunning rock formations. Day trip popular with tourists. Train plus cable car: €20-30 total. Hiking trails available.

Olympic Stadium:
Venue from 1992 Olympics. Can be visited externally or toured internally. Admission for tours: €10.

Beaches:
Barcelona has several Mediterranean beaches including Barceloneta Beach. Swimming and sunbathing free. Beach bars and restaurants available.

Barcelona Museum:
The city has numerous museums including Picasso Museum (€12), Maritime Museum, and History Museum, each with separate admission.

Nightlife and Entertainment:
Vibrant nightlife scene with bars, clubs, and live music venues. Prices vary widely.

Transportation: Barcelona has metro, buses, and trams. T-casual ticket (10 journeys) €11.35; unlimited day pass €8.50.

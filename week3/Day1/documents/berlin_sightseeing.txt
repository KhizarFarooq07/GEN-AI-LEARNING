Berlin Sightseeing and Major Attractions

Museum Island (Museumsinsel):
Located on an island in the River Spree, Museum Island is a UNESCO World Heritage site featuring five world-renowned museums: the Neues Museum, the Altes Museum, the Alte Nationalgalerie, the Bode Museum, and the Pergamon Museum. Admission: €14 per museum or €24 for a day ticket covering all five.

Brandenburg Gate:
An iconic 18th-century neoclassical monument and one of the most recognizable landmarks in Germany. Located at Pariser Platz, it is free to visit and offers excellent photo opportunities.

Berlin Wall Memorial:
The East Side Gallery is a 1.3-kilometer-long stretch of the Berlin Wall covered with murals by international artists. Open to the public for free. The Wall Memorial Site offers exhibitions about the history of the wall. Admission: €8.

Reichstag Building:
The seat of the German Parliament, featuring a stunning glass dome with panoramic views of Berlin. Free entry but advance booking required. Dome visit: Free.

Charlottenburg Palace:
A beautiful baroque palace set in a large garden. Admission: €12 for palace and gardens.

Tiergarten:
Berlin's largest park, featuring gardens, monuments, and the Berlin Zoo. Park entry free, Zoo admission: €18.

Checkpoint Charlie:
A famous crossing point between East and West Berlin during the Cold War. The checkpoint is open to the public, though entry to the museum costs €15.

Transportation: Berlin has an efficient public transportation system with U-Bahn (subway), S-Bahn (commuter trains), buses, and trams. A day pass costs €8-10.
